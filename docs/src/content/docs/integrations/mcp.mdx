Yahini has an [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server, so AI assistants that support MCP connectors can work with your projects. You can ask things like "Which keywords in my project have a brief but no draft?" or "Write briefs for the five highest-priority workbook keywords."

## Connect

<Steps>

1. In your AI app, add a custom connector (MCP server) with this URL:

   ```
   https://app.yahini.io/mcp
   ```

2. Your app opens Yahini. Sign in if asked.

3. Check the app's name and choose **Allow**.

</Steps>

The app now appears under **Connected apps** on your [Account](/account/settings/#connected-apps) page.

<Screenshot name="connected-apps" alt="Connected apps and the server URL on the Account page" />

:::caution[The app acts as you]
A connected app can do anything you can do in Yahini, including running agents that spend credits. Every paid tool states its cost in its description.
:::

## What it can do

**Read**

- List projects, keywords, the workbook, tasks and documents.
- Read a project's details and team, a brief or draft, brand intelligence, Search Console rankings and your account's plan and credits.
- Follow running work until it finishes.

**Act**

- Create a project, check or correct the website summary, and start keyword research.
- Add keywords to the workbook or hide them, set their priority or funnel stage, import keywords and fill in missing data.
- Edit tasks: status, due date, description, working title, secondary keywords and assignees.
- Generate and regenerate briefs, drafts and meta tags, and save edits to documents.
- Edit or generate brand intelligence.
- Change competitors, get their keywords and expand topics.
- Link a Search Console property, add its searches and find your keywords.
- Rename, archive and restore projects, and invite team members.

Each tool costs the same credits as the matching action in the app. See [Credits and agents](/account/credits/).
