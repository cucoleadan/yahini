## Sign up

<Steps>

1. Go to [app.yahini.io](https://app.yahini.io) and choose **Create an account**.

2. Enter your first name and work email, and accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. You can also continue with Google.

   <Screenshot name="signup" alt="Create your account: first name, work email and Google sign-up" />

3. Open the email Yahini sends you and click the link to confirm your address. There's no password to choose: from now on you sign in with an emailed link or with Google.

4. Answer the four short welcome questions: your role, how you heard about Yahini, how you handle content strategy today and why you signed up.

   <Screenshot name="welcome" alt="The welcome questions" />

</Steps>

:::tip[Invited to a team?]
If someone invited you to their project, open the link in your invitation email instead. It signs you in, or creates your account, and adds you to their team. See [Teams and roles](/account/team/).
:::

## Start your free trial

Agents run on credits, and a free account has none. So the first thing Yahini asks a new account to do is start a trial.

- The trial lasts **7 days** and starts on the **Starter** plan, billed monthly.
- You enter a card at checkout, but **nothing is charged until the trial ends**.
- You get the plan's full monthly credits straight away.
- Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

<Screenshot name="trial" alt="Home for a new account, with the free trial offer" />

You can switch plans once the trial starts. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing/).

## Sign in

On the [sign-in page](https://app.yahini.io/login), enter your email and choose **Email me a sign-in link**, or sign in with Google. If your account was created with a password, you can still sign in with it.

<Screenshot name="login" alt="Sign in with an email link, a password or Google" />
