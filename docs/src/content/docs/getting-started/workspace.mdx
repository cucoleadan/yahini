## Sidebar

| Item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | What needs you today, across every project. |
| **Projects** | Every project you own or have access to. Your recent projects are listed underneath. |
| **Content** | Every brief and draft across your projects. Your recent documents are listed underneath. |
| **Tasks** | Every workbook task across your projects. |
| **Agents** | What each agent does and what it costs. |
| **New project** | Starts [a new project](/getting-started/first-project/). |

Your account menu is at the bottom of the sidebar. It has **Account**, **Plans and credits**, **Get in touch**, **Report a bug**, **Take the tour**, the **Light**, **Dark** and **System** appearance options, and **Sign out**.

<Screenshot name="account-menu" alt="The account menu" />

The top bar shows your credit balance, the agents that are currently working and your notifications.

## Home

<Screenshot name="home" alt="Home: overdue and upcoming tasks, running agents and project progress" />

- **Needs you**: your overdue and upcoming tasks, work waiting for your review and runs that stopped.
- **Yahini is working**: the agents running right now.
- **Your tasks**: the open tasks assigned to you.
- **Projects**: each project's progress, from keywords to review, to the workbook, to published.

## Projects list

<Screenshot name="projects" alt="Projects list with keyword, workbook and brief counts" />

Search, filter by status (Ready, Generating, Needs attention or Archived) or by ownership, and sort by date or name. Select a project to open it.

## Inside a project

Every project has five tabs:

- **[Dashboard](/features-functionality/projects/)**: Search Console totals, workbook progress, upcoming tasks, project details and the team.
- **[Keywords](/features-functionality/keyword-research/)**: the researched keywords.
- **[Workbook](/features-functionality/workbook-strategy/)**: the keywords you chose to write about, as tasks.
- **[Brand](/features-functionality/brand-intelligence/)**: what Yahini knows about your business.
- **[Search Console](/features-functionality/search-console/)**: the searches your site appears for in Google.

The **⋯** menu next to the project name holds the project's settings: Search Console connection, topics and audience, competitors, topic expansion, renaming and archiving.

## Guided tours

The first time you open a page, a short tour points out what's on it. To see it again, choose **Take the tour** in the account menu.
