Brand intelligence is Yahini's profile of your business. Every brief and draft is written from it, so the content talks about your product, for your audience.

<Screenshot name="brand" alt="Brand intelligence: product summary, features, benefits, audience, pain points and use cases" />

## The six sections

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Product summary** | What the brand or product is, in a few sentences. |
| **Features** | Features or services offered. |
| **Benefits** | Advantages and outcomes for the customer. |
| **Target audience** | Who the ideal customer is. |
| **Pain points** | Problems the brand solves for its customers. |
| **Use cases** | How customers use the offering. |

Yahini fills these in during the project's first [keyword research](/getting-started/first-project/#step-2-keyword-research) run.

## Edit it yourself

Open the project's **Brand** tab.

- **Product summary**: choose **Edit**, change the text (up to 2,000 characters) and **Save**.
- **Lists**: type in **Add an item** to add one, up to 30 per section. Remove an item with its **×**.

Editing is free. The more accurate this is, the better your briefs and drafts.

## Generate or regenerate

Choose **Generate** (or **Regenerate** when every section has content) for **5 credits**. Yahini reads your website again and rewrites every section, replacing what's there. This can take a minute.

Only the project owner or a team admin or editor can generate brand intelligence, and the credits come from the owner's balance.
