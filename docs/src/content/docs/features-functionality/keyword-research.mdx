The **Keywords** tab lists every keyword Yahini found for the project. Your job here is to choose which ones to write about.

<Screenshot name="keywords" alt="Keywords table with volume, difficulty, priority and funnel stage" />

The tab has two views:

- **Available**: keywords you haven't added to the workbook or hidden.
- **Hidden**: keywords you set aside. Select them and choose **Unhide** to bring them back.

## What are the primary & secondary keywords?

Each row is a **primary keyword**: the main search a piece of content targets. Rows with an arrow expand to show **secondary keywords**. These are related searches with the same intent that you can cover in the same piece, instead of writing a separate page for each.

Yahini groups keywords into these clusters during research. In the workbook you can add or remove secondary keywords for each task.

<Screenshot name="keywords-secondary" alt="A keyword expanded to show its secondary keywords" />

## What is the search volume data?

**Volume** is how many times a month people search for the keyword, on average. Volumes below 10,000 are shown exactly, and higher ones are rounded, such as 12.5K.

In filters, volume is grouped as:

| Band | Monthly searches |
| --- | --- |
| Low | 0–100 |
| Medium | 101–1,000 |
| High | 1,001–10,000 |
| Very high | 10,000+ |

A low volume isn't a reason to skip a keyword. Specific, low-volume searches are often easier to rank for and closer to a purchase.

## What is Yahini's keyword difficulty score?

**Difficulty** (KD) is a 0–100 estimate of how hard it is to rank on the first page of Google. It takes into account the strength of the pages that rank now and your own site's authority.

| Label | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | 0–30 |
| Medium | 31–60 |
| Difficult | 61–75 |
| Hard | 76–90 |
| Very hard | 91–100 |

New or small sites usually do best with Easy and Medium keywords first.

## Priority scoring system

**Priority** combines several factors, including volume, difficulty, relevance to your business and funnel stage, into one rating. The higher the priority, the sooner it's worth targeting.

| Priority | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Very high | Your best opportunities. Start here. |
| High | Strong candidates. |
| Medium | Worth doing once the top keywords are covered. |
| Low | Lower return for now. |

The table is sorted by priority by default. You can change a keyword's priority yourself.

## Funnel stage mapping

The **funnel stage** says what the searcher wants, so each piece matches where they are:

| Stage | Searcher's intent | Typical content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TOFU** (top of funnel) | Learning about a problem | Guides, explainers, how-tos |
| **MOFU** (middle of funnel) | Comparing solutions | Comparisons, lists of options, use cases |
| **BOFU** (bottom of funnel) | Ready to choose | Product pages, alternatives, pricing, reviews |

The brief and draft are written for the keyword's funnel stage, so a keyword needs a stage before Yahini can write its brief.

## Source

The **Source** column shows where a keyword came from:

- **Yahini**: the project's keyword research or competitor keywords.
- A topic name: a [topic expansion](/features-functionality/competitors-and-topics/#expand-topics).
- **External**: imported from a CSV file.
- **Search Console**: added from [Search Console](/features-functionality/search-console/).

## Filter, sort and choose columns

- **Search** finds keywords by text.
- **Filters** narrows the list by funnel stage, priority, difficulty, volume, source and whether the keyword has a brief.
- Click a column heading to sort. Click again to reverse the order, and a third time to go back to the default.
- **Columns** shows or hides columns. Yahini remembers your choice.

## Pick keywords for the workbook

1. Tick the keywords you want to write about.
2. Choose **Add to workbook** in the page header.

Each keyword you add becomes a task in the [workbook](/features-functionality/workbook-strategy/). To set keywords aside, choose **Hide**.

With keywords selected, **Edit** sets the priority or funnel stage of all of them at once.

<Screenshot name="keywords-selected" alt="Three keywords selected, with Add to workbook, Hide and Edit" />

## Import and export

- **Export** downloads every keyword that matches your current filters as a CSV file.
- **Import** adds your own keywords from a CSV file, up to 200 at a time. Importing is free. Only the **Primary Keyword** column is required. Download the template from the import dialog, or use an export from another keyword tool: common headings such as `Keyword`, `Volume` and `KD` are recognised. Keywords already in the project are skipped. English keywords only.

<Screenshot name="keywords-import" alt="Import keywords from a CSV file" />

## Fill in missing data

Imported keywords and keywords from Search Console may not have a volume, difficulty or funnel stage. Select them and choose **Fill in**. Yahini looks up the missing data for **1 credit per 50 keywords**, up to 200 at a time. Only keywords with missing data are charged.
