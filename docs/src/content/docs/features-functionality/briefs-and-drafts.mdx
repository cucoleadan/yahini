For each workbook keyword, Yahini writes two documents:

- the **brief**, which plans the piece: its angle, outline, title, meta description and target length;
- the **draft**, the article written from the brief.

Both use your [brand intelligence](/features-functionality/brand-intelligence/), so they're written for your business.

## Generate a brief

1. Open a task in the [workbook](/features-functionality/workbook-strategy/).
2. Choose **Generate brief · 1 credit**.

The task shows **Writing the brief…** while Yahini works. You can keep browsing. When it's done, the task moves to **Brief ready** and **Open brief** appears.

:::note[A brief needs a funnel stage]
If the keyword has no funnel stage, set one in the task first, or select the keyword and use [Fill in](/features-functionality/keyword-research/#fill-in-missing-data).
:::

## Generate a draft

1. Open the task, or the brief.
2. Choose **Generate draft**, pick a **tone of voice** and confirm. This costs 1 credit.

The tones are informal (the default), formal, professional, casual, friendly and authoritative. When the draft is done, the task moves to **Draft ready**.

<Screenshot name="task-generate-draft" alt="Generate draft with a tone of voice" />

The brief needs a title, a description and a word count before a draft can be written. Yahini fills these in when it writes the brief.

<Screenshot name="brief" alt="A brief, with search appearance, task and keyword panels" />

<Screenshot name="draft" alt="The draft tab of the same document" />

The brief and the draft open on their own page, in two tabs. Beside the document you see:

- **Search appearance**: the meta title, meta description and target length;
- the **task**: its status, assignees and due date;
- the **keyword**: its volume, difficulty, funnel stage and secondary keywords.

### Edit

Click into the document to change anything. The toolbar has text styles (paragraph and headings), bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, inline code, lists, links, tables and dividers, plus undo and redo. Choose **Save** or press **Ctrl+S**. If you leave with unsaved edits, the page warns you.

### Regenerate with instructions

The **Generate** menu on the document page offers:

- **Write draft** or **Rewrite draft**: describe what to change and Yahini rewrites the draft. The brief stays as it is.
- **Regenerate brief**: describe what to improve and Yahini rewrites the brief and its metadata, replacing the current version.

Instructions need at least 200 characters, so Yahini knows what you want. Each regeneration costs 1 credit. Save or discard your own edits first.

<Screenshot name="brief-generate" alt="The Generate menu on the document page" />

### Meta title and description

In **Search appearance**, edit the meta title and description yourself for free. You can also have Yahini rewrite one or both for 1 credit.

### Export

**Export** offers:

- **Copy to clipboard**, formatted for Google Docs or Word;
- **Download Word (.docx)**.

<Screenshot name="brief-export" alt="The Export menu" />

The export starts with a table of the meta title, meta description, primary and secondary keywords and recommended word count. It includes unsaved edits.

## All your documents

**Content** in the sidebar lists every brief and draft across your projects. Filter by **Briefs** or **Drafts**, search titles and keywords, and sort by date or title.

<Screenshot name="my-briefs" alt="All briefs and drafts across projects" />

## Who can generate

The project owner and team admins and editors can generate documents. The credits come from the project owner's balance. See [Credits and agents](/account/credits/).
