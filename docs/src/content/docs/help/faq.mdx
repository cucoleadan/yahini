## Yahini couldn't read my website

Yahini needs to read your site publicly to understand your business. If it can't, for example because of a login wall, bot protection or a site that's mostly images, it asks you to describe the business, or to write the summary yourself. Keyword research then starts from your summary.

## A competitor says "Couldn't read the site" or "Site isn't in English"

Yahini can't use that competitor's content. [Change the competitor](/features-functionality/competitors-and-topics/#change-a-competitor) to another site. It's free, once a day per competitor.

## My keyword run failed

The credits are refunded automatically. Open the project and choose **Retry generation**, which is free.

## Generate brief is greyed out

Hover over the button to see why. The usual reasons:

- the keyword has no funnel stage: set one, or use [Fill in](/features-functionality/keyword-research/#fill-in-missing-data);
- another document is being written for this keyword: wait for it to finish;
- you're a viewer on the project: ask the owner or an admin for the editor role;
- you have unsaved edits on the document page: save or discard them first.

## It says I don't have enough credits

The action needs more credits than the paying account has. On a shared project, that's the owner's account. Nothing was charged. Upgrade, or wait for your monthly refill. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing/).

## I can't create another project

You've reached your plan's project limit. Archive a project you no longer need, or upgrade.

## Search Console shows no data

- Check that the linked property matches the project's website: **⋯ › Search Console connection**.
- New sites can take a few days to collect data in Google.
- If Yahini asks you to reconnect, Google has stopped sharing your data. Choose **Reconnect with Google**.

## Where are the keywords I hid?

On the Keywords tab, switch to **Hidden**.

## Can I write in languages other than English?

You choose your project's country and language when you create it, and research targets that market. Keyword import supports English keywords only.

## Can I undo a regeneration?

No. Regenerating a brief replaces the current version. Copy or export the document first if you want to keep it.
