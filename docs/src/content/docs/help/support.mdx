---
title: Contact support
description: How to get help, report a bug or suggest a feature.
---

In the app, open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar:

- **Get in touch** for a question, billing and credits, or a feature idea;
- **Report a bug** if something isn't working. Say what you did, what happened and what you expected.

Yahini sends the page you were on with your message and replies to your account email.

To see a page's guided tour again, choose **Take the tour** in the same menu.

## Improve these docs

Every page has an **Edit page** link at the bottom. It opens the page on [GitHub](https://github.com/cucoleadan/yahini), where you can suggest a change.
