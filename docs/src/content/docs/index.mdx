Yahini is a content workspace for websites that grow through search. You give it your website and a few competitors. It finds the searches worth writing about, plans each piece and writes it in your brand's voice. Then it shows how your pages perform in Google Search Console.

<Screenshot name="home" alt="Yahini's home page: overdue and upcoming tasks, running agents and project progress" />

## How it works

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="1. Research" icon="magnifier">
		Yahini reads your site and your competitors, then finds, scores and groups the keywords your site can win.
	</Card>
	<Card title="2. Plan" icon="list-format">
		Pick the keywords you want to write about. Each one becomes a task in your workbook, with a status, assignees and
		a due date.
	</Card>
	<Card title="3. Write" icon="pencil">
		Generate a brief that plans the piece, then a draft written from the brief. Edit both and export them to Word.
	</Card>
	<Card title="4. Track" icon="rocket">
		Connect Search Console to see clicks, impressions and rankings, and to add the searches you already appear for.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Key ideas

- **Project**: one website you research and write for, with its own competitors, country and language.
- **Keyword**: a search worth targeting. Each one has a volume, difficulty, priority and funnel stage, and related secondary keywords grouped under it.
- **Workbook**: the keywords you chose to write about. Each one is a task.
- **Brief** and **draft**: the plan for a piece and the article written from it.
- **Agents and credits**: each piece of AI work is an agent with a fixed credit cost. Failed work is refunded.
