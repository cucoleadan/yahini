Each piece of AI work in Yahini is done by an **agent**, and each agent has a fixed credit cost. You always see the cost on the button before you start.

<Screenshot name="agents" alt="Agents: keyword research, brand intelligence, topic expansion, competitor keywords, briefs, drafts and more" />

## What each agent costs

| Agent | What it does | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Keyword research](/getting-started/first-project/) | Reads your site and competitors, then finds, scores and groups the searches worth writing about. | **20** per run. Retrying a failed run is free. |
| [Brand intelligence](/features-functionality/brand-intelligence/) | Learns your positioning, products and audience. | **5** per run, or fill it in yourself for free. |
| [Topic expansion](/features-functionality/competitors-and-topics/#expand-topics) | Adds keywords for a feature or theme your list is missing. | **10** per topic |
| [Competitor keywords](/features-functionality/competitors-and-topics/#get-a-competitors-keywords) | Adds the searches a competitor gets traffic from that you don't have yet. | **5** per competitor. Changing a competitor is free, once a day. |
| [Content brief](/features-functionality/briefs-and-drafts/) | Plans one piece for a keyword. | **1** per brief or regeneration |
| [Content draft](/features-functionality/briefs-and-drafts/#generate-a-draft) | Writes a first draft from the brief, in the tone you choose. | **1** per draft or rewrite |
| [Meta title and description](/features-functionality/briefs-and-drafts/#meta-title-and-description) | Writes the snippet people see for your page in search results. | **1** per rewrite |
| [Find my keywords](/features-functionality/search-console/#find-my-keywords) | Adds the relevant searches your site already appears for in Search Console. | **1** per 50 searches checked |
| [Fill in data](/features-functionality/keyword-research/#fill-in-missing-data) | Completes the volume, difficulty and funnel stage of keywords you added yourself. | **1** per 50 keywords. Only missing data is charged. |

These are free: creating projects, correcting the website summary, editing anything yourself, importing keywords, adding Search Console searches, changing competitors and connecting Search Console.

## Who pays

Credits come from the **project owner's** balance, even when a team member starts the work. When you work on a project someone shared with you, the buttons say the credits are charged to the project owner.

## Failed work is refunded

If an agent fails, its credits go back to the balance they came from.

## Running out

If a balance is too low for an action, Yahini tells you what the action needs and what's available. Nothing is started or charged. From there you can upgrade your plan. Upgrading adds the extra credits right away.

Your balance is always in the top bar. Click it for your usage and the next refill date.
