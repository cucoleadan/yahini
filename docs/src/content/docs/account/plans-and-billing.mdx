## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Credits per month | Projects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | $19 | $171 ($14.25/month) | 50 | 1 |
| **Growth** | $39 | $351 ($29.25/month) | 200 | 5 |
| **Pro** | $79 | $711 ($59.25/month) | 1,000 | 25 |

Yearly billing gives you 12 months for the price of 9. Credits refill every month on both monthly and yearly plans.

The project count is the number of projects you own that aren't archived. Projects shared with you don't count.

## Free trial

Every plan starts with **7 days free** and the plan's full monthly credits. Your card is charged when the trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

## Change plan

Open **Plans and credits** from the account menu.

- **Upgrading**, to more credits or from monthly to yearly, applies right away. You get the extra credits immediately and pay the prorated difference.
- **Downgrading**, to fewer credits or from yearly to monthly, applies at your next renewal.

## Manage billing

**Manage billing** opens the billing portal, where you can cancel, update your card and download invoices.

If you cancel a paid plan, you keep it until the end of the period, then move to Free with the credits you have left. Cancelling during the trial ends it with no charge.

## Credits

- Credits refill every month. Unused credits don't carry over.
- You can redeem a promo code on the [Account](/account/settings/#redeem-a-code) page.

See [Credits and agents](/account/credits/) for what each action costs.

## Lifetime deal

Accounts with a lifetime deal keep it as it is. They have unlimited projects and the monthly credits included in the deal, and can't add a subscription.
