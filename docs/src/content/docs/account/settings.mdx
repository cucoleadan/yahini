Open **Account** from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar. It shows your credits, how many projects you have against your plan's limit, and your plan.

<Screenshot name="account" alt="The Account page" />

## Profile

Change the name Yahini uses for you. You sign in with an emailed link or with Google.

## Appearance

In the account menu, choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. System follows your device's setting.

## Redeem a code

If you have a promo or lifetime-deal code, enter it on the Account page to add its credits to your account.

## Connected apps

AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can use Yahini on your behalf. The **Connected apps** section lists the apps you've connected and shows the server URL to add. See [AI assistants (MCP)](/integrations/mcp/).

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** in the account menu.
